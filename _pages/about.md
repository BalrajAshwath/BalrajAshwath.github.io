My research interests broadly lie in Computer Vision and Machine Learning. Prior to joining VAL I was an undergraduate researcher at Image Processing and Analytics Lab (IPAL) in National Institute of Technology Karnataka (NITK). In my free time, I blog about algorithms that are related to my research interests.
