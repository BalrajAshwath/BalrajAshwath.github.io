I'm a Data Scientist / ML Researcher at Microland. I work on developing Machine Learning & Natural Language Processing pipelines for text-based analysis and learning for a cloud-based AIOps platform.

Previously, I was a Research Intern at [Video Analytics Lab (VAL)](http://val.serc.iisc.ernet.in/valweb), Indian Institute of Science where I worked with [Dr. Ram Prabhakar](https://sites.google.com/view/kramprabhakar/) and [Dr. Venkatesh Babu](http://cds.iisc.ac.in/faculty/venky/) on High Dynamic Range (HDR) Image Deghosting using CNNs.

I pursued my Bachelors in [Electronics and Communications Engineering](https://ece.nitk.ac.in/) from [National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal (NITK)](https://www.nitk.ac.in/). Here I did my thesis with [Dr. Shyam Lal](https://ece.nitk.ac.in/faculty/shyam-lal) in image segmentation methods on satellite imagery.

Research Interests
==================
Computer Vision, Deep Learning, Medical Imaging, Natural Language Processing
